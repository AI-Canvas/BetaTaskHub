AI Canvas in Splunk - Internal Task Review

Review the weekly beta tasks.

A pre-launch read-through for SOC analysts, admins, and SREs. Pick your track, then expand each week to read the tasks inline — worded exactly as participants will see them in the surveys, minus the feedback questions. Check that they make sense before we put this in front of customers.

What we need from you

Please go through the track that is relevant to your expertise. Flag tasks that are confusing, redundant, out of scope for that track, or missing something. — send notes/feedback straight to Maral (maraelli@cisco.com).

TrackSRE / DevOps Observability

This track tests AI Canvas in Splunk with SAIA 2.0 agent mode for observability workflows in your own production environment. You'll use AI Canvas (Search & Reporting > AI Canvas) to compose SPL, investigate log volume and errors, correlate events, and validate AI-generated results against raw data. Every non-trivial search will ask for your explicit permission before running, per agent mode's design. Tasks are read-only by design - nothing here will modify your environment.

Week 1

Orientation and free exploration

Get acquainted with AI Canvas in your environment. No multi-step or correlation work yet - we want a baseline of single-prompt interactions and the permission/drill-back loop.

4 tasks~60 minIncludes free exploration
1

Task 1 of 4: Generate SPL for a known issue

~15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to surface recent errors in a part of your environment you administer.
What to do
Open a new AI Canvas. Ask it - in your own words - to help you look at the errors happening in a part of your environment you care about. You decide the scope (which index, sourcetype, service), the time window, and how you want the results broken down. Approve the search when you're satisfied with the SPL.
2

Task 2 of 4: Source volume anomaly

~15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to spot indexes or sources whose log volume looks unusual right now versus a recent baseline.
What to do
Open a new AI Canvas. Describe the volume question in your own words. You pick the baseline window that's meaningful for your environment (a day, a week, a month) and the threshold for what counts as unusual. Approve the search when ready.
3

Task 3 of 4: Free exploration

Free exploration ~15 min
Goal
Spend 15 uninterrupted minutes using AI Canvas the way you would naturally use it for an observability question you have today. There is no right answer - pick an index, service, deployment, or recent incident and just try it.
What to do
Open Splunk in your production environment. Navigate to Search & Reporting and then AI Canvas. Start a new canvas. Type a natural-language question about something in your environment you actually care about. Let the agent respond. If it proposes a search, approve it or reject it as you would in real work. Repeat with a follow-up prompt if you want.
4

Task 4 of 4: Drill back to raw events

~15 min
Goal
Take any summary card from the previous three tasks and drill back to the raw events behind it. Confirm the summary matches reality.
What to do
From any of the cards in your canvas, click into the underlying events or ask the agent to show you the raw events that produced the summary. Scan the events. Return to the prior canvas view.
Week 2

Deeper agentic workflows and free exploration

Move from single-prompt interactions to multi-step agentic workflows. Test the agent's ability to chain steps and refine answers through follow-up prompts.

4 tasks~60 minIncludes free exploration
1

Task 1 of 4: Multi-step error-to-deploy correlation

~15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to find your noisiest service right now and then look for a deployment, config change, or other change event nearby that might explain it.
What to do
Start a new canvas. Describe the two-step investigation in your own words. You pick the time window, the data sources for change events (CI/CD logs, deploy notes, ticket index, whatever your team uses), and the definition of noisy. Approve searches as the agent proposes them.
2

Task 2 of 4: Iterate via follow-up prompts

~15 min
Goal
Build a search through the agent, then refine it across 3-4 follow-up turns. We want to see how it holds context.
What to do
Open a new canvas. Ask an opening question about errors or activity in a service or index you care about. Then push it through at least three follow-up turns - narrowing time, changing how it's grouped, adding or removing a filter. Phrase each follow-up however feels natural; the point is not the words but whether the agent keeps cumulative context.
3

Task 3 of 4: Validate AI output against raw

~15 min
Goal
Pick the most surprising or counterintuitive result from this week's tasks and validate it against raw data.
What to do
Find a result from the previous tasks that seemed off, or unexpected, or just worth confirming. Drill back to raw events. Read at least 5 actual events. Decide whether the AI's summary was accurate.
4

Task 4 of 4: Free exploration - your agentic workflow

Free exploration ~15 min
Goal
Spend 15 minutes trying any agentic workflow you'd use for routine ops. Multi-step, follow-up prompts, agent-proposed next steps - anything that goes beyond a single Q-and-A.
What to do
Pick a routine ops task: triage a noisy host, follow up on a recent deploy, check the health of a service. Use AI Canvas as your investigation surface. Use follow-up prompts liberally.
Week 3

Multi-source correlation and long-running steps

Test cross-source workflows and long-running steps that don't return immediately. This is where the canvas's persistence matters most.

4 tasks~55 min
1

Task 1 of 4: Cross-source correlation

~15 min
Goal
Ask AI Canvas to correlate evidence across two or more indexes/sourcetypes (e.g., application logs + infra metrics, or app errors + access logs).
What to do
In a new canvas, ask the agent to investigate a scenario that requires looking at two distinct data sources in your environment. Approve searches as needed. Look for whether the agent stitches the evidence into one narrative.
2

Task 2 of 4: Optional - ITSI index query (only if you have ITSI)

~10 min
Goal
If you have ITSI installed, ask AI Canvas to query data inside your ITSI indexes. Important: per Lizzy's guidance, AI Canvas can read ITSI indexes but cannot interact with ITSI episodes or invoke ITSI's AI skills in the beta - this task is read-only against ITSI's underlying data.
What to do
If you don't have ITSI, skip this task and mark it 'Skipped' in the feedback. If you do, ask the agent to query something straightforward from your ITSI indexes.
Skip unless: ITSI is installed in your environment. If this doesn't apply to you, skip it.
3

Task 3 of 4: Compose a saved search or dashboard via agent

~15 min
Goal
Ask AI Canvas to draft an SPL search you would actually save (or a dashboard panel you might add). Note: don't actually save it in production unless you want to - the test is the drafting flow, not the persistence.
What to do
Describe a saved-search-worthy use case in plain language. Have the agent compose it. Iterate until you'd be willing to save it. Optionally copy the SPL into a notepad outside Splunk to preserve it.
4

Task 4 of 4: Track a long-running step

~15 min
Goal
Start a step that takes a while to complete (a wide time-range search, a heavy correlation) and use the canvas to monitor it while doing other work.
What to do
Kick off a search that you know will take a while in your environment - you pick the scope. While it runs, start another question in the same canvas. Observe how the canvas tracks both.
Week 4

Production reality and critique

Use AI Canvas the way you would for real work, then critique it against your usual workflow. This is the week where we want unvarnished feedback.

4 tasks~60 min
1

Task 1 of 4: Real-incident-style multi-prompt investigation

~20 min
Goal
Pick a real ops question from your queue or recent history and investigate it end-to-end in AI Canvas. Use as many prompts as needed.
What to do
Choose a question you would actually have to answer this week (a real ticket, a recent incident, a performance question). Use AI Canvas as your investigation surface from start to finish. Try to stay in AI Canvas as long as it's useful - we want to see where it falls short before you reach for manual SPL.
2

Task 2 of 4: Have AI propose next steps - accept or reject with reason

~15 min
Goal
Get AI Canvas to recommend next investigation steps based on what's already in your canvas, and decide which ones you'd actually act on.
What to do
Continuing from a previous task (or in a fresh canvas with some results in it), ask AI Canvas in your own words to recommend where to look next. For each suggestion it gives, decide: would you actually do that? Capture your reasoning in a notepad alongside.
3

Task 3 of 4: Manual versus agentic comparison

~15 min
Goal
Take a question you've already answered manually this week and ask AI Canvas the same question. Compare time-to-answer, accuracy, and confidence.
What to do
Pick a recent investigation where you already know the answer. Ask AI Canvas the same question fresh. Time how long it takes. Compare its answer to yours.
4

Task 4 of 4: Capture concerns and feedback

~10 min
Goal
Capture anything that bothered you, surprised you, or would block you from using AI Canvas in real work.
What to do
Use the open-ended response below to capture concerns. Be specific - 'the SPL was wrong' is less useful than 'the SPL used eventtype= when there's no eventtype in my data, and it didn't recover when I told it that'.

Every non-trivial search will ask for your explicit permission before running. Tasks are read-only - no settings are modified.

TrackSOC / Security Analyst

This track tests AI Canvas in Splunk with SAIA 2.0 agent mode for security investigation workflows in your own production environment. You'll use AI Canvas (Search & Reporting > AI Canvas) to triage alerts, enrich entities, build incident timelines, and propose response actions. Per the AI Canvas PRD, the canvas is designed around human-in-the-loop approval for any action - we will not auto-execute SOAR, Cisco actions, or any containment without your explicit approval. Tasks are read-only or propose-only - no actual containment is executed.

Week 1

Orientation and free exploration

Get acquainted with AI Canvas for triage. Establish a baseline of single-prompt security interactions.

4 tasks~60 minIncludes free exploration
1

Task 1 of 4: Alert triage

~15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to help you triage what's high-severity in your environment right now.
What to do
In your own words, ask AI Canvas to surface the high-severity activity worth your attention. You decide what 'high-severity' means in your environment, what time window to look at, and how you want it broken down. Approve the search.
2

Task 2 of 4: Enrich one entity

~15 min
Goal
Pick one entity (user, IP, domain, host) from a previous task and use AI Canvas to build context around it.
What to do
Pick an entity. Ask AI Canvas - however you'd ask a teammate - to help you understand what's happening with that entity. You decide what context matters (recent activity, associations, geo, history). Approve searches.
3

Task 3 of 4: Free exploration

Free exploration ~15 min
Goal
Spend 15 minutes using AI Canvas to investigate a recent alert, event, or hunch in your environment.
What to do
Open AI Canvas. Pick an alert from your queue or a recent investigation question. Type a natural-language prompt. Approve searches as needed.
4

Task 4 of 4: Drill back from a summary

~15 min
Goal
Take any AI-generated summary from this week and validate it against raw events.
What to do
Drill back from a summary card to raw events. Read the actual events. Decide whether the summary is accurate.
Week 2

Deeper agentic security workflows

Multi-step triage and an introduction to SOAR action proposals with human-in-the-loop approval.

4 tasks~60 minIncludes free exploration
1

Task 1 of 4: Multi-step alert triage

~15 min
Goal
Pick one notable event and let AI Canvas chain through multiple queries to build full triage context for you.
What to do
Pick a notable event from your queue. Ask AI Canvas to help you triage it - phrase it however you would naturally describe the work to a teammate. Use follow-ups to push the agent deeper where you'd normally dig.
2

Task 2 of 4: Propose a SOAR action (HITL approval required)

~15 min
Goal
Have AI Canvas propose a SOAR or response action for an entity in your investigation. Per the PRD, human-in-the-loop confirmation is required before ANY action is taken. Do not actually execute. Walk through the approve/adjust/reject flow.
What to do
Pick an entity from a previous task. Ask the agent what action it would recommend. Look at the parameters (scope, target, risk). Decide whether you'd approve. Do NOT actually approve in production - just walk the flow.
Skip unless: Your environment has SOAR integrated, OR you can simulate the decision flow. If this doesn't apply to you, skip it.
3

Task 3 of 4: Reference an AI suggestion's underlying evidence

~15 min
Goal
For any AI-generated suggestion or finding, confirm you can see the evidence behind it.
What to do
Pick a suggestion from the previous tasks. Ask the agent to show the evidence and reasoning. Drill into raw data to confirm.
4

Task 4 of 4: Free exploration - agentic threat hunting

Free exploration ~15 min
Goal
Spend 15 minutes trying an agentic threat-hunting prompt. Multi-step, exploratory, hypothesis-driven.
What to do
Pick a threat hypothesis you've been wanting to investigate. Use follow-up prompts to refine. Let the agent propose where to look next.
