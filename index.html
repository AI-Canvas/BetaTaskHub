AI Canvas in Splunk - Customer Beta

AI Canvas in Splunk Beta program.

Welcome. This beta is the first round of evaluative research on AI Canvas with Splunk customers using their own production environment. Over four weeks, you’ll work through a small set of weekly tasks designed to surface how the agent behaves on real observability, security, and admin work — not on staged data.

What we’re learning

We’re testing whether AI Canvas with SAIA 2.0 agent mode genuinely helps you do your job — or whether it gets in the way. Specifically, we want to see how naturally you can phrase real work in your own words, where the agent’s reasoning holds up against your own data, and where trust, control, and recovery break down.

Your job during the beta is to use it like you’d use any other tool in your stack — on questions you actually have, in your own production environment, and to push on it when it doesn’t hold up. Tasks are read-only by design. Nothing here will modify your environment.

TrackSRE / DevOps Observability

This track tests AI Canvas in Splunk with SAIA 2.0 agent mode for observability workflows in your own production environment. You'll use AI Canvas (Search & Reporting > AI Canvas) to compose SPL, investigate log volume and errors, correlate events, and validate AI-generated results against raw data. Tasks are read-only by design - nothing here will modify your environment.

Week 1

Orientation and free exploration

Get acquainted with AI Canvas in your environment. Start small with 3 simple Observability tasks and 1 short self-exploration session.

4 tasks~60 minIncludes free exploration
Open Week 1 survey
1

Generate SPL for a known issue

15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to surface recent errors in a part of your environment you administer or care about.
Instructions
Open a new AI Canvas. Ask it - in your own words - to help you look at the errors happening in a part of your environment you care about. You decide the scope, the time window, and how you want the results broken down.
2

Ingest anomaly check

15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to spot indexes or sources whose log volume looks unusual right now versus a recent baseline.
Instructions
Open a new AI Canvas. Describe the volume question in your own words. You pick the baseline window that's meaningful for your environment (a day, a week, a month) and the threshold for what counts as unusual.
3

Drill back to raw events

15 min
Goal
Take any AI-generated summary card in your canvas and drill back to the raw events behind it. Confirm the summary matches reality.
Instructions
From any of the cards in your canvas, click into the underlying events or ask the agent to show you the raw events that produced the summary. Scan the events. Return to the prior canvas view.
4

Free exploration

Free exploration 15 min
Goal
Spend 15 uninterrupted minutes using AI Canvas the way you would naturally use it for an observability question you have today. Pick an index, service, deployment, or recent incident and just try it.
Instructions
Start a new canvas. Type a natural-language question about something in your environment you actually care about. Let the agent respond. Repeat with a follow-up prompt if you want.
End of week. The last section of the survey asks you to rate the value of this week overall and share an open-ended note about what helped or got in the way.
Week 2

Deeper agentic workflows and free exploration

Move from single-prompt interactions to multi-step agentic workflows. Test the agent's ability to chain steps and refine answers through follow-up prompts.

4 tasks~60 minIncludes free exploration
Open Week 2 survey
1

Multi-step error-to-deploy correlation

15 min
Goal
Use AI Canvas to find your noisiest service right now and then look for a deployment, config change, or other change event nearby that might explain it.
Instructions
Start a new canvas. Describe the two-step investigation in your own words. You pick the time window, the data sources for change events (CI/CD logs, deploy notes, ticket index, whatever your team uses), and the definition of noisy.
2

Iterate via follow-up prompts

15 min
Goal
Build a search through the agent, then refine it across 3-4 follow-up turns. We want to see how it holds context.
Instructions
Ask AI Canvas questions about errors or activity in a service or index you care about. Then push it through at least three follow-up turns - narrowing time, changing how it's grouped, adding or removing a filter. Phrase each follow-up however feels natural; the point is not the words but whether the agent keeps cumulative context.
3

Validate AI output against raw

15 min
Goal
Pick the most surprising or interesting result from this week's tasks and validate it against raw data.
Instructions
Find a result from the previous tasks that seemed off, or unexpected, or just worth confirming. Drill back to raw events. Read at least 5 actual events. Decide whether the AI's summary was accurate.
4

Free exploration - your agentic workflow

Free exploration 15 min
Goal
Spend 15 minutes trying any agentic workflow you'd use for routine ops. Multi-step, follow-up prompts, agent-proposed next steps - anything that goes beyond a single Q-and-A.
Instructions
Pick a routine ops task: triage a noisy host, follow up on a recent deploy, check the health of a service. Use AI Canvas as your investigation surface. Use follow-up prompts liberally.
End of week. The last section of the survey asks you to rate the value of this week overall and share an open-ended note about what helped or got in the way.
Week 3

Multi-source correlation and long-running steps

Test cross-source workflows and long-running steps that don't return immediately. This is where the canvas's persistence matters most.

4 tasks~55 min
Open Week 3 survey
1

Cross-source correlation

15 min
Goal
Ask AI Canvas to correlate evidence across two or more indexes/sourcetypes (e.g., application logs + infra metrics, or app errors + access logs).
Instructions
In a new canvas, ask the agent to investigate a scenario that requires looking at two distinct data sources in your environment. Look for whether the agent stitches the evidence into one narrative.
2

Optional - ITSI index query (only if you have ITSI)

10 min
Goal
If you have ITSI installed, ask AI Canvas to query data inside your ITSI indexes. Important: AI Canvas can read ITSI indexes but cannot interact with ITSI episodes or invoke ITSI's AI skills in the beta - this task is read-only against ITSI's underlying data.
Instructions
If you don't have ITSI, skip this task and mark it 'Skipped' in the feedback. If you do, ask the agent to query something straightforward from your ITSI indexes.
3

Compose a dashboard panel via agent

15 min
Goal
Ask AI Canvas to draft and compose a dashboard panel you would actually use.
Instructions
Describe a complex recent use case in plain language. Have the agent compose it. Iterate until you'd be willing to save it in an existing dashboard.
4

Continue an investigation from a saved Report

15 min
Goal
Start where you'd normally start - In Dashboard Studio dashboard, Alert with triggered results or from a saved Report - then bring that result into AI Canvas to keep investigating with the agent.
Instructions
Open a saved Report or a dashboard you already use. Send the result or dashboard panel into AI Canvas - use whatever in-product affordance is exposed for adding the report's results to a canvas. Once it's in the canvas, ask the agent follow-up questions to extend the investigation. If you can't find the affordance, capture that as feedback and try copying the SPL or describing the result in a new canvas as a fallback.
End of week. The last section of the survey asks you to rate the value of this week overall and share an open-ended note about what helped or got in the way.
Week 4

Production reality and critique

Use AI Canvas the way you would for real work, then critique it against your usual workflow. This is the week where we want unvarnished feedback.

5 tasks~75 minFinal SUS + exit feedback
Open Week 4 survey
1

Real-incident-style multi-prompt investigation

20 min
Goal
Pick a real ops question from your queue or recent history and investigate it end-to-end in AI Canvas. Use as many prompts as needed.
Instructions
Choose a question you would actually have to answer this week (a real ticket, a recent incident, a performance question). Use AI Canvas as your investigation surface from start to finish. Try to stay in AI Canvas as long as it's useful - we want to see where it falls short before you reach for manual SPL.
2

Have AI propose next steps - accept or reject with reason

15 min
Goal
Get AI Canvas to recommend next investigation steps based on what's already in your canvas, and decide which ones you'd actually act on.
Instructions
Continuing from a previous task (or in a fresh canvas with some results in it), ask AI Canvas in your own words to recommend where to look next. For each suggestion it gives, decide: would you actually do that? Capture your reasoning in a notepad alongside.
3

Manual versus agentic comparison

15 min
Goal
Take a question you've already answered manually this week and ask AI Canvas the same question. Compare time-to-answer, accuracy, and confidence.
Instructions
Pick a recent investigation where you already know the answer. Ask AI Canvas the same question fresh. Time how long it takes. Compare its answer to yours.
4

Optional - Collaborate on a canvas with a teammate

15 min
Goal
If another member of your team is also in the beta, collaborate with them on a canvas. Pick something you'd normally work through together.
Instructions
Pick a task you'd normally tackle with a teammate. Ask another team member who's also in the beta to join you, then share your canvas with them: copy the canvas link and send it to them. Work the task together in the shared canvas. If no teammate is available, skip this task and mark it 'Skipped' in the feedback.
5

Capture concerns and feedback

10 min
Goal
Capture anything that bothered you, surprised you, or would block you from using AI Canvas in real work.
Instructions
Think aloud and be specific - 'the SPL was wrong' is less useful than 'the SPL used eventtype= when there's no eventtype in my data, and it didn't recover when I told it that'. Once you are done recording your final feedback, enter a summary of your feedback below.
End of week. The last section of the survey asks you to rate the value of this week overall and share an open-ended note about what helped or got in the way.
System Usability Scale (SUS). Week 4 closes with the standard 10-item SUS questionnaire so we can benchmark AI Canvas against industry usability norms. About 5 minutes.
