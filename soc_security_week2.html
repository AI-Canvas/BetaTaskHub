SOC / Security Analyst · Week 2

Deeper agentic security workflows

Multi-step triage and an introduction to SOAR action proposals with human-in-the-loop approval.

Internal review

This page mirrors what a beta participant sees for this week — the intro and the tasks — without the feedback questions. Please work through the tasks for your track and flag anything that doesn't make sense, is missing, or wouldn't work in your environment.

How this works. This week is about one hour of tasks. For each task you'll see the goal and what to do — but not a specific prompt to type. There's no right or wrong way to prompt the AI. Phrase each task however feels natural and see how AI Canvas responds. If AI Canvas misreads what you meant, that's useful signal too.
Record your screen. In the live beta, participants screen-record (with audio) while they work, then upload the file via a secure Box link. The short walkthrough below is the same one they'll get.
4 tasks · about one hour
1

Task 1 of 4: Multi-step alert triage

~15 min
Goal
Pick one notable event and let AI Canvas chain through multiple queries to build full triage context for you.
What to do
Pick a notable event from your queue. Ask AI Canvas to help you triage it - phrase it however you would naturally describe the work to a teammate. Use follow-ups to push the agent deeper where you'd normally dig.
Phrase it your way
Use your own words. There's no right or wrong way to prompt the AI. Phrase this however feels natural to you and see how AI Canvas responds. Don't worry about getting it perfect - if AI Canvas misreads what you meant, that's useful signal too.
2

Task 2 of 4: Propose a SOAR action (HITL approval required)

~15 min
Goal
Have AI Canvas propose a SOAR or response action for an entity in your investigation. Per the PRD, human-in-the-loop confirmation is required before ANY action is taken. Do not actually execute. Walk through the approve/adjust/reject flow.
What to do
Pick an entity from W2T2. Ask the agent what action it would recommend. Look at the parameters (scope, target, risk). Decide whether you'd approve. Do NOT actually approve in production - just walk the flow.
Phrase it your way
Use your own words. There's no right or wrong way to prompt the AI. Phrase this however feels natural to you and see how AI Canvas responds. Don't worry about getting it perfect - if AI Canvas misreads what you meant, that's useful signal too.
Skip unless: Your environment has SOAR integrated, OR you can simulate the decision flow. If this doesn't apply to you, skip it.
3

Task 3 of 4: Reference an AI suggestion's underlying evidence

~15 min
Goal
For any AI-generated suggestion or finding, confirm you can see the evidence behind it.
What to do
Pick a suggestion from W2T1-W2T3. Ask the agent to show the evidence and reasoning. Drill into raw data to confirm.
Phrase it your way
Use your own words. There's no right or wrong way to prompt the AI. Phrase this however feels natural to you and see how AI Canvas responds. Don't worry about getting it perfect - if AI Canvas misreads what you meant, that's useful signal too.
4

Task 4 of 4: Free exploration - agentic threat hunting

Free exploration ~15 min
Goal
Spend 15 minutes trying an agentic threat-hunting prompt. Multi-step, exploratory, hypothesis-driven.
What to do
Pick a threat hypothesis you've been wanting to investigate. Use follow-up prompts to refine. Let the agent propose where to look next.
Phrase it your way
